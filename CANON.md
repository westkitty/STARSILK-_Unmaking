# Canon Boundary Note — Starsilk: The Unmaking Engine

This document separates established Starsilk principles from the experimental inventions of this simulation, as required by the build contract.

## Known Starsilk principles (honored)

- **"Stars don't burn; they surrender."** The visual logic of collapse is surrender, withdrawal, and structural failure — light and structure fail inward rather than exploding into flame. There is no conventional fiery supernova or outward fireball.
- Starsilk is an operative cosmic force, not decorative yarn or generic electricity. It is rendered as distinctive **azure, barcode-like ribbons/filaments** with deliberate line rhythm and dimensional depth.
- The gesture of pulling Starsilk is **causally connected** to the star's fate: grip, stretch, resistance, threshold, then surrender.
- A star's **collapse**, the resulting **black hole / absence node**, and the expanding **negative-space lattice** are distinct, readable states.
- Extraction is a **simulation affordance**, not a declaration that any specific canon character possesses that ability.

## Experimental simulation inventions (not canon)

This instrument is **self-contained and noncanonical**. No verified source material was consulted or presumed. The following are inventions of this experimental instrument and must not be treated as canon:

- The five stellar systems (**SYSTEM I–V**) and their neutral designations; no canon proper names, factions, or historical incidents are used.
- The connection graph, stress threshold, chain-reaction delay, and collapse timing are arbitrary simulation parameters chosen for legibility, not established lore.
- All object counts (5 stars, 13 planets, 5 moons) are simulated inventory only. They distinguish **exact simulated objects destroyed** from unknowable lives lost; no authoritative casualty or population count is claimed or portrayed.
- The black-hole / absence rendering (lensing contours, dark spans, displaced starlight traces) is an invented visual metaphor for "missing cosmic structure," not a depiction of any canon phenomenon.
- Audio (stellar drone, filament tension, collapse transient, silence) is an invented synthesis, not tied to any canon soundscape.
